ENVIRONMENTAL ISSUE

Construction Debris Affecting Urban Drainage & Land Use

Construction waste can block drainage, occupy valuable land and increase environmental pressure.

Construction activity and responsible waste management
🏗️ Build responsibly — manage waste properly.
💧 Keep drains clear — prevent blockages.
♻️ Recover materials — reduce waste.
01 — INTRODUCTION

Why Does It Matter?

Construction and demolition activities generate materials such as concrete, bricks, soil, wood and metal. Poorly managed debris can enter drainage systems, occupy valuable land and create environmental concerns.

Key idea: Responsible construction-waste management helps protect drainage systems, land resources and urban environments.
